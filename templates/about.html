THE POMODORO PROTOCOL

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. It uses a timer to break work into intervals, traditionally 25 minutes in length, separated by short breaks.

HOW IT WORKS

  • 1. Choose a task you want to focus on.
  • 2. Set the timer to 25 minutes (FOCUS mode).
  • 3. Work on the task without interruptions.
  • 4. When the timer rings, take a 5-minute break (REBOOT mode).
  • 5. After four focus sessions, take a longer 15-minute break (SLEEP mode).

WHY IT WORKS

By breaking your work into manageable chunks, you reduce mental fatigue and procrastination. The strict time limits create a sense of urgency, while the regular breaks prevent burnout and keep your mind fresh.
